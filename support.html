Gavel support

Help with the Gavel iPhone app and widget

Contact

Email ian@sysalert.co.uk with a short description of the problem, your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps. You'll usually get a reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Open Gavel and tap Sign in with eBay. You sign in on eBay's own page, so Gavel never sees your password. You stay signed in for about 18 months.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit then Add Widget, search for Gavel, choose a size and tap Add Widget.

Why isn't the widget up to date?

iOS decides when widgets refresh, usually about every 15 minutes and sooner just after an auction ends. Opening the app refreshes everything straight away. The time in the widget's top corner shows when it last updated.

The widget says "Unlock Pro in the app"

Medium and large widgets are part of Gavel Pro. The small widget, showing your watch list, is free.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open the Pro tab and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro is a one-off purchase and isn't a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sign-in fails or my listings don't load

Go to Settings in the app, tap Sign Out of eBay, then sign in again. Check that the right eBay site is selected. If it still fails, email a screenshot of the error.

How do I remove Gavel's access to my eBay account?

Tap Sign Out of eBay in the app's Settings. You can also withdraw access at any time in your eBay account settings, under third-party app access.
