Gavel privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Overview

Gavel keeps your eBay data on your iPhone. It is an iPhone app and home-screen widget, made and run by Ian Moreton, that shows the items you're selling and watching on eBay, with their prices, bids and time left. Gavel is not made by or affiliated with eBay.

This policy explains what the app and its widget access, where that information is kept, and how to remove it. It covers only this app, not eBay itself; eBay's own handling of your data is set out in eBay's User Privacy Notice.

Information the app accesses

The app never receives your eBay password. You sign in on eBay's own sign-in page, and eBay gives the app a token that lets it read your listings.

The app does not access your name, address, email, payment details, messages, purchase history or location. eBay's details of a sale can include the buyer's eBay user ID; the app ignores it and does not store it.

How it's used and stored

Your listings, sales and watch list stay on your iPhone. Information is used only to show them in the app and widget.

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain, shared only between the app and its own widget.
  • Listings, seen-bid counts and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone, shared only with its widget.
  • Thumbnails are held in memory while the widget draws and are not saved.
  • The eBay sign-in page opens in a private browsing session that keeps no cookies after it closes.

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use your information to build profiles.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The app makes these connections:

  • eBay's sign-in page, to sign you in
  • The Gavel token service, to collect and renew your sign-in tokens (see below)
  • eBay's Trading API, to fetch your listings, recent sales and watch list
  • eBay's image servers, to download listing thumbnails
  • Apple's App Store, if you buy or restore Gavel Pro

The token service

To keep the app's eBay keys secret, the step that turns your eBay sign-in into tokens, and later renews them, goes through a small service run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers. It passes your request to eBay and passes eBay's reply straight back to your iPhone. It does not store or log your tokens, and it never sees your listings, sales or watch list, which the app fetches directly from eBay. As with any web service, Cloudflare handles your iPhone's IP address to deliver the request and may keep it briefly for security and abuse prevention; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Gavel Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer receives no payment details or personal information, only confirmation from Apple that Pro is unlocked on your Apple account. See Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can remove everything at any time by tapping Sign Out of eBay in the app. That deletes the sign-in tokens, cached listings, sales, watch list and seen-bid counts from your iPhone straight away. Your Pro purchase is kept by Apple, and you can restore it at any time.

  • Cached listings are replaced each time the app or widget refreshes, so only the latest copy is kept.
  • Deleting the app removes its stored listings and settings. iOS can keep Keychain entries after an app is deleted, so sign out first if you want the tokens gone too.
  • Revoking access on eBay: you can withdraw the app's access at any time in your eBay account settings, under third-party app access. The tokens then stop working.
  • Closing your eBay account: the app keeps no copy of your eBay data anywhere except your own iPhone, and the token service keeps nothing, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Signing out or deleting the app removes it from your phone.

Security

All connections, to eBay and to the token service, use encrypted HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone Keychain, which iOS encrypts and protects with your device passcode.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, which matches the minimum age for an eBay account.
