eBay Selling widget privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Overview

eBay Selling keeps everything on your iPhone and talks only to eBay. It is a personal iPhone app and home-screen widget, made and run by Ian Moreton, that shows the listings you're selling on eBay with their prices, bids and time left.

This policy explains what the app and its widget access, where that information is kept, and how to remove it. It covers only this app, not eBay itself; eBay's own handling of your data is set out in eBay's User Privacy Notice.

Information the app accesses

The app never receives your eBay password. You sign in on eBay's own sign-in page, and eBay gives the app a token that lets it read your listings.

The app does not access your name, address, email, payment details, messages, buyers' details, purchase history or location.

How it's used and stored

Everything stays on your iPhone; the app has no servers of its own. Information is used only to show your listings in the app and widget.

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain, shared only between the app and its own widget.
  • Listings, seen-bid counts and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone, shared only with its widget.
  • Thumbnails are held in memory while the widget draws and are not saved.
  • The eBay sign-in page opens in a private browsing session that keeps no cookies after it closes.

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use your information to build profiles.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The only connections the app makes are directly from your iPhone to eBay:

  • eBay's sign-in and token services, to sign you in and renew access
  • eBay's Trading API, to fetch your active listings
  • eBay's image servers, to download listing thumbnails

The developer cannot see your tokens or listings, because none of it passes through anything the developer runs.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can remove everything at any time by tapping Sign Out of eBay in the app. That deletes the sign-in tokens, cached listings and seen-bid counts from your iPhone straight away.

  • Cached listings are replaced each time the app or widget refreshes, so only the latest copy is kept.
  • Deleting the app removes its stored listings and settings. iOS can keep Keychain entries after an app is deleted, so sign out first if you want the tokens gone too.
  • Revoking access on eBay: you can withdraw the app's access at any time in your eBay account settings, under third-party app access. The tokens then stop working.
  • Closing your eBay account: the app keeps no copy of your data anywhere except your own iPhone, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Signing out or deleting the app removes it from your phone.

Security

All connections to eBay use encrypted HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone Keychain, which iOS encrypts and protects with your device passcode.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, which matches the minimum age for an eBay account.
